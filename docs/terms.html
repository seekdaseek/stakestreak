The game

StakeStreak is a skill-based social accountability game. Players create or join a "pot" by staking an equal amount of SOL, held in escrow by the service. The pot creator sets a daily rule and one or more required check-in times. Once a pot starts, every active member must check in within the grace window of each required time, measured in the member's own local timezone. Members who miss any required check-in on any day are eliminated and forfeit their stake. When the pot ends, surviving members split the entire pot equally, minus a 3% service fee. Outcomes depend entirely on each player's own daily actions.

Starting and filling

A pot starts once at least 3 members have joined. Others may join during a 24-hour window from creation, after which the pot locks. If fewer than 3 members join within 24 hours, the pot does not start and all stakes are automatically refunded.

Fees and purchases

A 3% service fee is deducted from the total pot at settlement. A streak freeze may be purchased (0.015 SOL) to cover a single missed day and restore an eliminated member, limited to 2 per pot. Freeze purchases are final and non-refundable.

Refunds

Before a pot starts, any member may leave and receive a full refund of their stake via the app. If a pot fails to fill within 24 hours, all stakes are refunded automatically. Once a pot starts, stakes are committed and cannot be withdrawn.

Notifications

New-pot push notifications are optional and opt-in. You may enable or disable them at any time in the app or your phone settings.
