StakeStreak

Stake. Survive. Split the pot.

StakeStreak is a skill-based accountability game for the Solana Seeker. Put SOL on the line, commit to a daily habit, and check in every day to survive. Members who miss a day are eliminated — and the survivors split the whole pot at the end.

How it works

  • Create a pot — set your rule, your stake, how many days, and one or more daily check-in times.
  • Friends join — everyone stakes the same amount. Pots are public to discover, or share an invite link.
  • Check in daily — check in within each time window, in your own timezone. Miss one, you're out.
  • Survivors win — when the pot ends, everyone still standing splits the whole pot. A 3% service fee applies.

Fair by design

Every check-in is a signed wallet action recorded on-chain. If a pot doesn't fill with at least 3 players in 24 hours, all stakes are automatically refunded. Outcomes depend entirely on your own daily actions — this is accountability, not chance.

Built for the Solana Seeker using Mobile Wallet Adapter and Seed Vault. Optional push notifications let you know when new pots open to join.
