Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

StakeStreak is a skill-based accountability game on the Solana blockchain. This policy explains exactly what we store and why.

What we store

  • Your public wallet address — to track which pots you belong to and credit your check-ins and payouts.
  • Check-in timestamps — to verify you met the pot's daily requirement.
  • Pot membership and history — which pots you created or joined, and their outcomes.
  • Your timezone offset — so daily check-in windows are matched to your local time. We store the numeric offset only, not your location.
  • A push-notification token — only if you turn on "notify me when new pots drop." This is a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token used solely to send you new-pot alerts. Turn the toggle off and the token is deleted.

What we do NOT collect

No names, emails, contacts, GPS location, photos, or analytics tracking. No ads. We never have access to your private keys — every transaction is signed by your own wallet via Mobile Wallet Adapter.

Push notifications

Notifications are opt-in. If you enable them, we use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver alerts when a new joinable pot is created. We store only the device token needed to reach your phone. Disabling notifications in the app removes the token from our servers; you can also revoke notification permission in your phone settings.

On-chain data

Stakes, payouts, and refunds are Solana transactions and are public by nature of the blockchain. This is outside our control and inherent to how the app works.

Where data is stored

Off-chain data (check-ins, membership, tokens) is stored on servers located in Germany (EU).

Deleting your data

You can delete your off-chain records anytime from inside the app: open "How it works" and tap "Delete my account & data." This removes your check-in history and finished-pot records from our servers. On-chain transactions are permanent and cannot be deleted. You cannot delete while you are in a running pot.
